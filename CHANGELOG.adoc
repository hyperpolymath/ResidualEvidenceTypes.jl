=== [0.1.0] - 2026-09-30

==== Added

* `Case`, `candidates`, `isconsistent`, `witness` and `refine`.
* `decide`, returning a `Verdict` with a supporting candidate and a
  counterexample.
* `identify`, returning an `IdStatus` and the distinct values.
* The `signed-integer-v1` model: `signed_integer_case`, `row` and
  `explorer_table`.
* A test suite: Aqua, behaviour tests, and agreement with the Agda explorer
  table from `residual-evidence-types`.
